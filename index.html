Our Purpose & Vision

Positioning Youth as Co-Creators of Our Economic Future.

The BELNASA Youth Forum is a regional collective of ambitious young leaders across Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia, and South Africa. Our purpose is to mainstream youth voices in development decision-making processes and drive critical dialogue, youth action, and socioeconomic initiative across the subregion.

Originating as a strategic liaison connecting young leaders on the ground with international development stakeholders—including the World Bank Group—BELNASA is building a sustainable, professionalized regional institution that mobilizes capital, skills, policy research, and peer-to-peer knowledge transfer.

Our Vision

To instill renewed hope among African youth by empowering them as co-creators of a subregion that enables inclusive economic futures.

Our Mission

To convene spaces, streamline youth policy inputs, and mobilize tangible opportunities for poverty alleviation and innovation.

Regional Stakeholder Engagement

Our operational focus centers on five core pillars designed to unlock opportunities and amplify youth participation across Southern Africa.

01

Opportunity Sharing

Identifying and promoting capital, skills, jobs, fellowships, and enterprise resources for youth across the region.

Building leadership pipelines and skills transfer between emerging youth leaders and established industry experts.

03

Streamlining Youth Inputs

Synthesizing diverse grassroots youth perspectives to inform regional development priorities and institutional partners.

Convening high-impact regional summits, hackathons, and capacity-building workshops across all five countries.

Policy Engagement

Driving youth-led policy research and advocating for equitable, cooperative economic growth in the subregion.

Concept Note

About the Summit

The Imagine Ventures Youth (IVY) Summit will equip aspiring and emerging entrepreneurs from Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia and South Africa (BLNS countries) with practical knowledge, tools, and funding insights to transform their innovative ideas into viable business ventures. Moving beyond ideation, it serves as a collaborative platform to learn from industry professionals and showcase youth-led success stories. By fostering networking and providing freely accessible digital resources, the IVY Summit drives sustainable entrepreneurial growth across the region.

Organizers

Who Are We?

The BELNASA Youth Forum (BELNASA)

A self-organized collective of young people from Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia, and South Africa. The forum's purpose is to mainstream and empower the voices of diverse youth in socioeconomic decision-making processes while driving critical dialogue, action, and initiative within their respective communities.

Imagine Scholar

A transformative youth educational development program based in South Africa. Its thriving alumni community acts as a self-sustaining network and a collective force for community-focused social impact; launching ventures, driving local social and economic impact, and leading the next generation of youth.

Primary Objective(s)

  • • Equip aspiring and emerging entrepreneurs with practical knowledge and tools to transform innovative ideas into viable business ventures.
  • • To provide insights into business strategy, planning, funding and fundraising opportunities that support business development and sustainability.
  • • To create a platform for knowledge sharing through experienced entrepreneurs, industry professionals and creative individuals who can share practical experiences and lessons.
  • • To encourage young entrepreneurs to move beyond ideation by developing actionable strategies, identifying opportunities and taking meaningful steps towards entrepreneurship.
  • • To showcase youth-led initiatives, innovative ideas and entrepreneurial success stories that can inspire further development and collaboration.

Goal(s)

  • • Empower young people from Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia, and South Africa, with knowledge, skills and resources to transform business ideas into viable and sustainable ventures.
  • • Create shareable resources including session recordings that will be digitally shared with the youth on the relevant platforms (including YouTube) after the summit at no cost.
  • • Foster entrepreneurial growth through funding opportunities, mentorship, networking and meaningful collaboration beyond the summit.
Primary Target Audience

Aspiring entrepreneurs, young innovators, creative and emerging for-profit and non-profit business owners from Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia, and South Africa.

Secondary Target Audience

Established entrepreneurs, investors, mentors, and industry professionals.
